# Class presentation and teacher feedback

This is a preparation guide, not evidence that a presentation or conference occurred.

## Suggested four-minute demonstration

**Alan, about 90 seconds.** Explain why matching one-sided slopes matters. Demonstrate the secant approaching the tangent to x² at (3,9), then contrast the corner of |x−2|. Show how the same derivative idea links position, velocity and acceleration. Give the counterexample v<0 and a<0: speed increases.

**Jeff, about 90 seconds.** Explain “candidates, then proof.” Show why endpoints matter for f(x)=x³−3x on [−2,2]. Demonstrate the open box and justify x=10/3 rather than stopping at a zero derivative. Briefly identify the economics and illustrative biological models.

**Both, about one minute.** Open the files and identify `math.js`, `scenes.js`, `timing.js` and the shared player. Each person demonstrates a real parameter change, predicts its effect and checks the result. Explain which work used assistant help and which work each student performed. Do not fill in contribution records for actions that did not occur.

## Questions to be ready for

- Why does differentiability imply continuity, but continuity not imply differentiability?
- Can positive acceleration mean slowing down?
- Why does f′=0 fail to prove a maximum? What happens at a corner?
- What conditions does the Extreme Value Theorem require? Why is [0,∞) a different case?
- What makes an optimization model valid in its stated domain?
- Why is the drug model illustrative rather than measured biological evidence?
- How do recorded clips map to animation beats? What is still awaiting review?
